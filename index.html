🌻 Garden Defense

A garden tower-defense game for Android.

Plant your defenses, hold the lawn, and stop the walker waves before they cross your garden. Build a loadout of seed packets — shooters, walls, frost, mortars and more — clear the campaign, then take on Hard, Insane and Hell modes, or see how long you can survive in Endless.

Features

  • Campaign levels across day and dusk gardens, with star ratings
  • A growing roster of plants and walker types, including upgrade plants
  • Four difficulty modes plus an Endless survival mode
  • Up to four local player profiles with a local leaderboard
  • Optional Google sign-in to sync your progress across your own devices through your own Google Drive

About

Garden Defense is a personal project, shared with friends and staff for testing and fun. It is not published on any app store.

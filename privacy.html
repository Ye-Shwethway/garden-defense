Garden Defense — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Garden Defense is an Android game developed by Ye Shwe Thway as a personal project. This policy explains what data the game handles and what it does not.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress — profiles, unlocked levels, star ratings, settings and leaderboard entries — is stored locally on your device. It stays on your device unless you choose to use the optional cloud sync described below. Uninstalling the game removes this local data.

Optional Google sign-in and cloud sync

Cloud sync is optional. The game is fully playable without signing in.

If you choose to sign in with a Google account, the game saves a copy of your game progress file in your own Google Drive, inside the app's private application data folder (appDataFolder). This folder is hidden, is only accessible to Garden Defense, and is never visible to the developer or to other players. The only Google permission the game requests is the drive.appdata scope — access to that private folder and nothing else. The game cannot see, read, or modify any other file in your Google Drive.

Signing in is used only to identify your Google account for this sync and to move your save file between your own devices. Your save data is never sent to the developer or to any other third party.

Data the developer collects

None. The game contains no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking tools. The developer does not collect, receive, sell, or share any personal data from players.

Your control

  • You can revoke the game's access to your Google account at any time from your Google Account permissions page (myaccount.google.com/permissions).
  • You can delete the synced save data from Google Drive: Drive Settings → Manage apps → Garden Defense → Delete hidden app data.
  • You can play entirely offline and locally without ever signing in.

Children

Garden Defense is a general-audience game. As described above, it does not collect personal data from any player, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
